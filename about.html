Diya Gifts Gallery began with one simple question — where do you find a gift that truly feels personal? Here's the story of how that question became a brand.

Meet the Founder

Hi, I'm Diya Patel.

Creative Entrepreneur Founder, Diya Gifts Gallery

I'm a creative entrepreneur, and the founder of Diya Gifts Gallery. My entrepreneurial journey began with a simple challenge: while searching for a birthday gift for a friend, I realised how difficult it was to find something truly unique, meaningful, and personal.

That experience led me to reflect on how many others likely face the same difficulty. Rather than settling for conventional options, I decided to create a solution — a brand dedicated to transforming emotions into thoughtfully customised gifts and hampers.

What started as a small idea has evolved into a business where every order is carefully designed to celebrate special occasions and create lasting memories.

Diya Founder & Creative Director
